Why us

When the door is always open, training fits your life.

Most "24 hour gyms" in the Asheville area still close on holidays, restrict access during off-hours, or run a smaller equipment set after dark. NC Open Gym is genuinely open 24/7/365 — same equipment, same floor, same access, every hour of the day.

You enter with a personal barcode through the RhinoFit app. No staffed-hours window. No waiting on someone to unlock the door. If you train at 4:30am before a hospital shift, or 10:30pm after putting kids to bed, the gym is ready.

Built for

Built for shift-workers and early risers.

  • Nurses, EMS, and hospital staff coming off overnight shifts
  • Parents who only get gym time after kids are down
  • Lawyers, founders, and remote workers fitting training into a long workday
  • Athletes who train twice a day and need an after-hours option
  • Travelers landing late at AVL who still want to lift

FAQ

Common questions.

Is NC Open Gym actually open 24 hours?

Yes — 24/7/365. Members and pass holders use a personal barcode to enter at any time, including holidays.

How does access work after-hours?

You scan your personal barcode at the door using the RhinoFit app. No front-desk staff is required.

Is the gym safe at night?

The facility is monitored, well-lit, and entry is logged. Members appreciate that the off-peak hours are quiet but still secure.

How close is it to I-26?

One mile from exit 37 — fast in/out for anyone driving from Asheville, Hendersonville, or further out.
