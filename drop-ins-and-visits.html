Drop-in questions.

How does barcode access work for drop-ins?

After purchase you'll receive instructions to set up RhinoFit and a personal barcode for entry. Once set up, you scan in any time, day or night.

Is the 7-day pass for locals?

The 7-day pass is for non-residents only. Locals trying NC Open Gym for the first time should use the 5 Days for $5 trial.

Are there guest hours?

No — open 24/7/365.

Is parking free?

Yes, free on-site parking.
