Why us

A serious gym for Arden, not a corporate chain.

NC Open Gym sits at 565 Long Shoals Rd, Suite 201 — easy access whether you're coming from Long Shoals, Hendersonville Road, or the Long Shoals/I-26 interchange.

It's owner-operated, members-know-each-other, and stocked for actual training: squat racks, lifting platforms, full barbell and dumbbell sets, rowers, bikes, sleds, and rigs. No towel-folders. No upsells. No 14-day cancellation runaround.

Built for

Built for Arden residents who take training seriously.

  • Locals who want a real gym a few minutes from home
  • Athletes who don't want to drive into downtown Asheville
  • Couples and families who want to train together
  • Personal trainers who want a real floor for their clients
  • Remote workers slotting training into the day
