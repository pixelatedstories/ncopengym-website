FAQ

Common questions.

What hours is NC Open Gym open?

We're open 24/7/365. Members enter using personal barcode access, anytime.

Are there contracts or sign-up fees?

No contracts and no sign-up fees. Bi-monthly and semi-annual billing options. Cancel any time.

Do you have classes?

NC Open Gym is an open-gym format — no class conflicts. Bring your own plan, hire a trainer, or follow the whiteboard programming.

What kind of equipment do you have?

Functional fitness equipment: squat racks, platforms, full dumbbell & kettlebell sets, barbells, rowers, assault bikes, sleds, rigs, plyo, GHD, climbing rope.

Can I drop in if I'm visiting Asheville?

Yes — single-day passes are $15 and a 7-day guest pass is $50 for non-residents.

Is parking free?

Yes, free on-site parking, one mile from I-26 exit 37.
