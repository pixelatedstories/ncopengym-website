Why us

A real squat. A real deadlift. A real platform.

Most commercial gyms in the Asheville area aren't built for powerlifting. The bars wobble, the plates rattle, and someone yells when you drop a lockout.

NC Open Gym keeps a chalk-tolerant floor with proper power racks, lifting platforms, calibrated and bumper plates, and the kind of ceiling height that doesn't ruin a heavy clean. Train your peaking block without apologizing.

Built for

Built for lifters peaking for a meet.

  • Power racks with j-cups and proper safety pins
  • Multiple deadlift platforms — drop and reset
  • Calibrated plates available for accuracy
  • Chalk-tolerant floor
  • 24/7 access for late-night singles or early-morning openers

FAQ

Yes — on the lifting platforms with bumper plates. Use sense and respect the equipment.

A power bar suitable for heavy training is on the floor. Bring your preferred bar if you're prepping for a meet.

Is chalk allowed?

Yes.

Can I bring a coach?

Yes — bring your own coach, or work with one of our independent trainers.
