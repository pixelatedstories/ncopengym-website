Why us

CrossFit-style training. Open-gym format.

You don't have to give up barbells, kettlebells, rowers, and rigs just because you don't want a 5pm class. NC Open Gym is built like a CrossFit-style facility — full rig, platforms, all the bells and balls — but runs as an open gym.

Members typically follow CompTrain, Mayhem, Functional Bodybuilding, PRVN, their own coach, or the in-house programming. Train when it works for your day.

Built for

Built for CrossFit-style athletes.

  • Full rig with pull-up stations and rings
  • Olympic lifting platforms with bumpers
  • Kettlebells, dumbbells, wall balls, slam balls, jump ropes
  • Rowers, assault bikes, ski erg, sleds
  • Plyo boxes, GHD, climbing rope
  • No class times — train when you want

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this an actual CrossFit affiliate?

No — NC Open Gym is independent and runs an open-gym format, not affiliated with CrossFit Inc. The equipment and style of training will feel familiar.

Can I do remote programming here?

Yes — most members do. CompTrain, Mayhem, FBB, PRVN, your coach, etc. all work fine on the floor.

Are there workout-of-the-day options?

Yes — daily programming on the whiteboard if you want one.

Do you have an assault bike and rower?

Yes — both. Plus ski erg.
