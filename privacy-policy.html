Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

NC Open Gym ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website ncopengym.com. This page describes how we collect and handle information when you use our site.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — such as your name, email address, and phone number — when you:

  • Fill out a contact form or chat widget
  • Purchase a membership or drop-in pass through RhinoFit
  • Sign up for a trial

We also collect standard web analytics data (pages visited, browser type, referral source) through tools described below.

Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • RhinoFit — membership and payment management. Your payment and membership data is handled by RhinoFit.
  • GoHighLevel / LeadConnector — contact forms and chat widget. Messages submitted are stored in GoHighLevel.
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's servers. Google may log the request.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and support requests
  • To process memberships and payments
  • To send relevant updates about NC Open Gym (you can opt out at any time)

Data retention and your rights

We keep your information as long as needed to provide our services. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us at the email or phone number below.
