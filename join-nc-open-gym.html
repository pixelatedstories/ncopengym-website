FAQ

Membership questions.

Are there contracts?

No. Cancel any time. Bi-monthly billing is on a rolling 2-week cycle.

Are there sign-up fees?

None. The price you see is the price you pay.

What's the difference between bi-monthly and semi-annual?

Bi-monthly bills every 2 weeks. Semi-annual is six months paid up front, with a built-in discount ($100–$300 depending on plan).

Can I freeze my membership?

Reach out at contact or call (828) 490-1223 — we'll work with you.

Do you offer punch cards?

Yes — the 10-Visit Pass is $110 and never expires while active.
