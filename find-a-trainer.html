Certified Personal Trainer

Jo Ovenell

Jo is a NASM certified personal trainer, a NESTA certified heart rate performance specialist and a rotational sports specialist. As a former college athlete, she obtained her Bachelor's of Science at Columbus State University in Georgia.

During her training, she focuses on mobility, functional training and weight loss. Her passion is to create a holistic and disciplined training that will last her clients a lifetime. During her free time, you'll most likely find her spending time with her dog or watching Hallmark Christmas movies.

ACE Certified Personal Trainer & NBHWC Board Certified Health Coach

Maria Borisevich

Maria believes that building a strong body shouldn't come at the expense of your peace of mind. As an ACE Certified Personal Trainer, she specializes in mobility, strength training, and functional movement designed to help you move better in everyday life.

Maria brings a deep sense of empathy to every session, knowing that starting or returning to an exercise routine can feel intimidating; her goal is to meet you exactly where you are with zero judgment.

Beyond the gym floor, Maria is a Board Certified Health Coach with extensive experience supporting clients managing obesity, prediabetes, and diabetes, as well as those navigating life on recently popular GLP-1 medications. She bridges the gap between fitness goals and everyday life. And when not nerding out on wellness, you can find her backpacking and hiking or hosting sober curious community events around Asheville.

Mobility Strength Training Health Coaching GLP-1 Support ACE Certified NBHWC

Licensed Massage Therapist & Certified Personal Trainer

Caitlin Anear

Caitlin helps active people get out of pain and back to training through movement retraining and targeted bodywork.

As a Licensed Massage Therapist and Certified Personal Trainer, she assesses posture, breathing, and movement patterns to fix the root cause — not just chase symptoms. Her work combines trigger point therapy and myofascial techniques with movement retraining to restore mobility, improve stability, and build a stronger, more resilient body.

She works with lifters, runners, and athletes recovering from injury or surgery, and anyone who wants to move better and train with confidence. With 20+ years in healthcare, Caitlin brings a steady, supportive approach focused on long-term results.

Injury Recovery Movement Retraining Massage Therapy Trigger Point Myofascial 20+ yrs Healthcare

ACE Certified Personal Trainer & Bujinkan Budo Taijutsu Instructor

Owen Hempton

Owen is a licensed instructor in Bujinkan Budo Taijutsu through the headquarters dojo located in Noda, Japan. He began his martial arts career in middle school training in Karate, and since then has trained in Judo and Kendo before discovering the Bujinkan in 2006.

After years of training and a stint in the United States Air Force, Owen moved to Asheville in 2018 and earned his Godan (5th degree black belt) in 2021. He currently holds the rank of Rokkudan (6th degree black belt).

Owen is passionate about teaching and approaches Bujinkan training holistically — studying not just the physical techniques, but the history and culture of Japan as well. During his free time, Owen likes to play video games and spend time with friends and loved ones.

Bujinkan Budo Taijutsu Martial Arts Self Defense 6th Degree Black Belt USAF Veteran
