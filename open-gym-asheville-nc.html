Why us

Open gym, the way it should be.

"Open gym" usually means a small window between classes. At NC Open Gym, the open floor is the entire model. There are no scheduled classes that take over the equipment. You walk in, scan, train.

Bring your own program. Follow your remote coach. Read the whiteboard programming. Lift with a friend. Move at your own pace. The floor is yours from the moment you scan in.

Built for

Built for self-directed athletes.

  • You already have a program from a remote coach
  • You're training for a meet, race, or event
  • You're past the class-based phase of your training
  • You want to lift heavy without waiting
  • You want quiet hours to focus, not a 6pm class roar

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you ever run classes?

No scheduled classes. Independent trainers occasionally work with clients on the floor, but they don't take over equipment or block use.

Can I bring my own programming?

Yes — that's the model. Most members do.

Is there in-house programming?

Yes — daily programming on the whiteboard if you want a workout to follow.

Will it ever be crowded?

Some hours are busier than others, but with 24/7 access there's always a slow window if you prefer a quieter floor.
