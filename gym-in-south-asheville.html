Why us

South Asheville training, without the downtown drive.

If you live in Biltmore Park, Skyland, or anywhere along Hendersonville Road, NC Open Gym is the closest no-nonsense training facility. You don't have to fight Asheville traffic to lift.

Members come from across South Asheville — and from Arden, Mills River, and Fletcher — for the same reasons: 24/7 access, real equipment, and a community that takes training seriously without taking itself too seriously.

Built for

Made for South Asheville locals.

  • Live near Biltmore Park or Skyland and want a quick drive
  • Need to train before work without commuting downtown
  • Want a serious lifting environment, not a class-based studio
  • Have a coach or program you bring with you
  • Need 24/7 access for shift work or odd hours

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does it take to get there from Biltmore Park?

Roughly 5–8 minutes via Long Shoals Rd or Hendersonville Rd, depending on traffic.

Is this near Asheville Outlets?

Yes — about 2 miles from the outlets at the Long Shoals / I-26 interchange.

Do you offer drop-ins for South Asheville locals?

Locals trying NC Open Gym for the first time should use the $5 trial. Drop-ins are best for visitors.

Is parking easy?

Yes — free, plenty of spaces, right outside the door.
